How a job works
  1. Find the car and type the number plate to verify it.
  2. Walk around the car and record any damage that’s already there.
  3. Take the before-photo, then start the wash.
  4. Take the after-photo and mark the wash complete.
  5. The customer pays on their phone. Cash is the fallback.

Work only on booked cars. A job belongs to whoever verified it.
